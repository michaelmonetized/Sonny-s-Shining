# SONNY'S SHINING

**An Original Screenplay**

---

FADE IN:

---

## ACT ONE: THE FALL

---

### SCENE 1

**INT. BERTIE'S BUSTLING BUBBLES - NIGHT**

*New Year's Eve. The bar throbs with jazz and laughter. Confetti drifts through cigarette smoke like snow in a fever dream.*

*SONNY TUFTS (30s, built like a man who throws heavy things for a living) sits at a corner booth, his bowling bag tucked beneath the table. Across from him: LUCY (late 20s, red hair, a smile that could start wars).*

*A countdown begins somewhere in the crowd.*

**CROWD**
Ten... nine... eight...

*Lucy's hand finds Sonny's. Her eyes sparkle.*

**LUCY**
You really think you can take Kewpie at the championship?

**SONNY**
Baby, I could beat that fat toad blindfolded.

**CROWD**
Seven... six... five...

**LUCY**
*(leaning closer)*
Promise me something.

**SONNY**
Anything.

**LUCY**
Win. I want to see his face when you—

*BERTIE (50s, Black, dignified even in a bartender's apron) appears at their table. He bends down, whispers something in Lucy's ear.*

*The countdown continues, but time seems to slow.*

**CROWD**
Four... three...

*Lucy's expression shifts. Something complicated crosses her face—fear? Excitement? She pulls away from Sonny.*

**SONNY**
Lucy? What did he—

**LUCY**
I have to go.

**SONNY**
What? It's almost midnight—

*She's already up, already moving toward the door behind the bar.*

**CROWD**
Two... one... HAPPY NEW YEAR!

*Champagne corks pop. Couples kiss. Confetti explodes.*

*Sonny watches Lucy disappear through the back door. Through the front window, he catches a glimpse of a STRETCH LIMOUSINE shaped like a giant bowling pin pulling away from the curb.*

*He knows that limo.*

*Bertie stands behind the bar, watching Sonny with a slow, cruel smile spreading across his face.*

**BERTIE**
*(laughing)*
Happy New Year, champ.

*He hurls a champagne glass at Sonny's head.*

*Sonny ducks. The glass SHATTERS against the wall behind him.*

**SONNY**
The hell is wrong with you?

*Bertie grabs another glass. Then a bottle. The WAITRESSES turn, gripping their brooms like weapons.*

**BERTIE**
Kewpie sends his regards.

*Sonny reaches into his bowling bag. Pulls out a BALL-SHINING TOWEL. Wraps it around his fist.*

**SONNY**
Then give him mine.

*WIDE SHOT: The bar becomes a battlefield. Waitresses swarm. Bottles fly. Sonny moves through them like a force of nature, using his towel to catch and return projectiles, bare-handing bottles and slinging them back like bowling pins.*

---

### SCENE 2

**INT. BERTIE'S BUSTLING BUBBLES - CONTINUOUS**

*The last waitress crumples. Sonny stands before the bar, breathing hard. Bertie backs up against the liquor shelf, out of ammunition.*

**BERTIE**
Now wait a minute—we can talk about this—

*Sonny vaults over the bar.*

**SONNY**
Where is she?

**BERTIE**
I don't know what you're—

*Sonny grabs him by the collar.*

**SONNY**
WHERE?

**BERTIE**
*(terrified)*
She went out back—through the alley—I don't know where they took her, I swear—

*Sonny stares at him for a long moment. Then smiles. It's not a nice smile.*

**SONNY**
Thanks for the tip.

*CUT TO:*

**EXT. ALLEY BEHIND BERTIE'S - CONTINUOUS**

*A TRASH CAN sits against the brick wall. We hear MUFFLED SCREAMING from inside.*

*PULL BACK to reveal: Bertie, folded in half, ass-first in the can. Sonny drags the can toward the curb, sets it down with the rest of the garbage.*

**SONNY**
*(dusting off his hands)*
Taking out the trash.

*He turns toward the dark alley. Somewhere in the distance: a WOMAN'S GIGGLE.*

---

## ACT TWO: THE CHASE

---

### SCENE 3

**EXT. ALLEY - NIGHT**

*Sonny moves through the narrow passage between buildings. Steam rises from grates. Shadows pool like oil.*

*At the far end of the alley, a SILHOUETTE—unmistakably Lucy—dives through a door on the right. Her GIGGLE echoes and fades.*

**SONNY**
Lucy!

*A door BURSTS OPEN on his left. CHARLIE (a massive, fire-engine-red CRAB with flowing blonde hair) scuttles into the alley. Atop his shell, controlling him like a puppet: a tiny FIELD MOUSE with a Charlie Chaplin mustache.*

**MOUSE**
*(in a tiny, squeaky voice)*
You're not going anywhere, big boy.

*Charlie's claws snap. Kitchen staff pour out behind him, hurling trash, boxes, pots and pans.*

**SONNY**
A crab. They sent a crab.

*He dodges a flying cleaver.*

**SONNY (CONT'D)**
I've officially seen everything.

---

### SCENE 4

**EXT. ALLEY - LATER**

*The kitchen staff lie scattered among the dumpsters. Sonny faces Charlie and his tiny puppeteer.*

*The mouse chatters commands. Charlie lunges with both claws.*

*Sonny sidesteps, grabs the crab's legs, and begins TYING THEM together like a ponytail.*

**MOUSE**
No! NO! What are you doing?!

*Charlie flails helplessly. Sonny lifts the bound crab over his head, aims for a STOCK POT that's way too small, and SLAMS him in headfirst.*

*The mouse screams as they're both wedged into the pot.*

*A GARBAGE TRUCK rumbles at the end of the alley, its compactor grinding.*

*Sonny winds up like a pitcher and HURLS the pot over the fence. It lands perfectly in the truck's maw.*

*CRUNCH.*

*A trail of fluid leaks from the back of the truck as it drives away.*

*Sonny watches it go. Then—*

**LUCY (O.S.)**
*(distant, laughing)*
—and I told him, I said—

*He looks UP.*

---

### SCENE 5

**EXT. ALLEY - LOOKING UP - CONTINUOUS**

*Four stories above, silhouetted against the FULL MOON: Lucy on a fire escape, chatting casually with an unseen figure.*

**SONNY**
Lucy! LUCY!

**DESI (O.S.)**
*(angry, from a window)*
KEEP IT QUIET DOWN THERE!

*A VASE hurtles from an apartment window, SMASHING on the concrete inches from Sonny's feet.*

*He looks up to see DESI (50s, slick hair, silk robe) glaring down from a third-floor window.*

**DESI**
You want noise complaints? I'LL GIVE YOU NOISE COMPLAINTS!

*He disappears. A moment later, a LAMP follows the vase.*

*Sonny grabs the fire escape ladder and begins to climb.*

---

### SCENE 6

**EXT. FIRE ESCAPE - NIGHT**

*A vertical battlefield. Sonny ascends through a rain of household objects—vases, lamps, window AC units, potted plants.*

*TENANTS lean out windows, hurling whatever they can find. Desi orchestrates from above, pointing and shouting.*

**DESI**
That's it! Hit him! HIT HIM!

*Sonny ducks a toaster. Catches a flower pot. Throws it back.*

*He reaches the top platform. Desi waits, a WIRE COAT HANGER gripped like a weapon.*

**DESI (CONT'D)**
You picked the wrong building, pal.

**SONNY**
*(eyeing the hanger)*
Nice weapon.

**DESI**
It was my mother's.

**SONNY**
I'm sure she'd be proud.

*They fight. It's brutal and ugly. Desi swings the hanger. Sonny catches his arm, twists—*

*—and SHOVES THE HANGER DOWN DESI'S THROAT.*

*Desi gurgles. Sonny hooks the hanger onto a CLOTHESLINE stretching across the alley.*

**SONNY (CONT'D)**
Going down?

*He PUSHES. Desi ZIPS across the alley on the clothesline, picking up speed, until—*

*He doesn't make it.*

*Sonny doesn't watch the landing. He's already climbing to the roof.*

---

### SCENE 7

**EXT. ROOFTOP - NIGHT**

*Empty. Dark. Wet from recent rain. Party debris scattered everywhere—but no people.*

*Sonny stands alone, catching his breath.*

*Then: a GIGGLE. Faint. Almost musical.*

*He runs across the rooftop, following the sound. Club music THUMPS louder and louder as he approaches the edge.*

*He looks down.*

*Four stories below: Lucy climbs into the back of Kewpie's bowling-pin limousine. It speeds off, takes two right turns, and disappears behind a building pulsing with neon.*

*A sign: BESSIE'S BOUNCING BALLROOM.*

*But first—another sign on the building directly across the street: TIPPI'S TANTALIZING TANGO. Fourth floor.*

*Sonny looks at the POWER LINES stretching between buildings. Looks at his bowling towel.*

**SONNY**
This is gonna hurt.

*He wraps the towel around the line and JUMPS.*

---

### SCENE 8

**INT. TIPPI'S TANTALIZING TANGO - NIGHT**

*CRASH!*

*Sonny EXPLODES through the fourth-story window, rolling across a floor covered in... interesting equipment.*

*Cages. Leather-clad massage tables. Chains hanging from the ceiling.*

*TIPPI (60s, severe, blonde, beautiful in a terrifying way) rises from a massage table, wearing something that would make a dominatrix blush.*

*Around her: CUSTOMERS in various states of undress, clutching stilettos and batons.*

**TIPPI**
Do you have an APPOINTMENT?

**SONNY**
*(standing, brushing off glass)*
I'm looking for the elevator.

**TIPPI**
The only way out of my studio is feet first.

*She snaps her fingers. Her customers attack, hurling their... equipment.*

**SONNY**
Why does everyone in this city throw things?

*He fights through the maze of leather and chains, dodging stilettos and "personal massage devices."*

---

### SCENE 9

**INT. TIPPI'S TANTALIZING TANGO - LATER**

*The customers are down. Tippi backs against a DANCE POLE, breathing hard.*

**TIPPI**
You don't know who you're dealing with.

**SONNY**
Lady, I stopped caring three floors ago.

*He grabs her. She struggles. He positions her at the base of the pole and—*

*—DRIVES HER ONTO IT.*

*From tooth to tailbone. Like a kebab.*

*Sonny walks to the shattered window. Looks at the sidewalk across the street.*

**SONNY (CONT'D)**
Special delivery.

*He THROWS. The pole—with Tippi attached—sails through the air like a javelin and lands with a distant THUNK.*

*Sonny turns to the elevator. Presses the button.*

*DING.*

---

### SCENE 10

**INT. ELEVATOR - CONTINUOUS**

*Sonny steps in. Yanks the crank to FREEFALL.*

*The elevator PLUMMETS.*

---

### SCENE 11

**INT. BESSIE'S BOUNCING BALLROOM - NIGHT**

*BOOM.*

*The elevator doors EXPLODE outward. Dust billows into the nightclub.*

*The music STOPS. Dancers freeze. Every head turns toward the ruckus.*

*Sonny emerges from the fog, scanning the crowd.*

*And there she is.*

*LUCY. At the bar. Champagne in hand. She locks eyes with Sonny—*

*—and SCOFFS.*

**LUCY**
Unbelievable.

*She sets down her drink and walks calmly out the front door.*

**SONNY**
Lucy, WAIT—

*BOUNCERS close in from all sides. BESSIE (40s, glamorous, African American, vinyl records under each arm) drags her crates to the front door, LOCKS it, and makes a show of SWALLOWING THE KEY.*

**BESSIE**
*(grinning)*
Nobody's going anywhere, sugar.

*She pulls a record from her crate and FLINGS it at Sonny's head like a frisbee.*

**BESSIE (CONT'D)**
Let's dance.

---

### SCENE 12

**INT. BESSIE'S BOUNCING BALLROOM - LATER**

*The bouncers are down. The cocktail tables are shattered. Red crowd-control ropes lie tangled across the floor like entrails.*

*Bessie backs against the locked door, clutching her last record.*

**BESSIE**
Stay back!

*Sonny walks toward her. Takes the record from her trembling hands.*

**SONNY**
Open wide.

**BESSIE**
Wha—

*He SHOVES the record into her mouth with such force that the vinyl SLICES through her cheeks—a Glasgow smile stretching from the corners of her lips to her earlobes.*

*She screams, but it comes out as a wet gurgle.*

*Sonny STOMPS on her back, folding her like a lawn chair. He grips her body like a bowling ball—thumb in the belly button, two fingers in the eye sockets—*

*—winds up—*

*—and LAUNCHES her through the glass front doors.*

*She lands in traffic. A bus finishes the job.*

*Sonny steps through the shattered doors just in time to see Lucy climbing into Kewpie's limo AGAIN. It peels off up the street.*

*A TAXI pulls up where the limo just was.*

*Sonny yanks open the driver's door.*

**SONNY (CONT'D)**
Out.

---

### SCENE 13

**EXT. CITY STREETS - NIGHT**

*MONTAGE: Sonny behind the wheel, weaving through oncoming traffic. Cars honk. Pedestrians dive. The limo is always just ahead, always just out of reach.*

*He's gaining.*

*He's almost there.*

*Then—from a cross street—a TOW TRUCK from "HARRY'S HOLY HAULERS" T-BONES him at full speed.*

*The taxi SPINS through a chain-link fence and into—*

---

### SCENE 14

**INT. HARRY'S HOLY HAULERS - AIRPLANE HANGAR - CONTINUOUS**

*—an old airplane hangar converted into a CHOP SHOP.*

*The taxi slides to a stop in a shower of sparks. Sonny crawls out, dazed.*

*MECHANICS emerge from between gutted vehicles, wielding PIPE WRENCHES and JACK HANDLES.*

*At the far end of the hangar: HARRY (50s, handlebar mustache, missing several teeth) stands on the bed of a tow truck, hurling MUFFLERS and SOCKET WRENCHES.*

**HARRY**
Wrong turn, friend.

**SONNY**
*(spitting blood)*
Story of my night.

---

### SCENE 15

**INT. HARRY'S HOLY HAULERS - LATER**

*The mechanics lie scattered among the car parts. Sonny approaches the tow truck.*

*Harry backs up, hands raised.*

**HARRY**
Look, man, I was just doing a job—Kewpie pays good—

*Sonny grabs the TOW HOOK on the WINCH CABLE.*

**SONNY**
Tell him yourself.

*He THROWS the hook. It punches through Harry's chest and out his back.*

*Harry looks down at the hook protruding from his sternum.*

**HARRY**
Oh.

*Sonny YANKS the cable. Harry SPINS, getting wrapped up like a mummy. Tighter. Tighter.*

*One final yank—like cracking a whip—*

*The spiraled cable SLICES Harry into perfect discs. They fall to the ground like bloody poker chips.*

*Sonny climbs onto the tow truck's crane arm, grabs the winch cable, and SWINGS out the hangar's back door.*

---

### SCENE 16

**EXT. IVY'S ILLUMINATING IMPORTS - WHARF - NIGHT**

*A maze of shipping containers and expensive cars. The only light comes from scattered, barely-functioning streetlamps. Shadows swallow everything else.*

*Sonny moves through the darkness. He can HEAR them—footsteps, whispers, the click of weapons—but he can barely SEE them.*

*At the end of the wharf: a TOWER. A crow's nest built from shipping containers, rising above the dock like a crooked finger.*

*At the top: IVY (60s, pearl necklace, designer dress, currently dropping ANTIQUE LAMPS toward Sonny's head).*

**IVY**
You're trespassing on private property!

*CRASH. A Tiffany lamp SHATTERS next to him.*

**SONNY**
*(climbing)*
Lady, the property damage I've done tonight could fund a small country.

*He dodges. Climbs. Dodges. Climbs.*

---

### SCENE 17

**EXT. IVY'S TOWER - TOP - CONTINUOUS**

*Sonny pulls himself over the edge. Ivy backs against the railing, holding her last lamp like a shield.*

**IVY**
Do you have any idea how much these are worth?

**SONNY**
About to find out.

*He grabs the lamp from her hands, tips her head back, and SHOVES IT DOWN HER THROAT.*

*While she gags, he reaches INTO her guts, finds the electrical cord, and RIPS it out.*

*He plugs it into a nearby outlet.*

*Ivy begins to CONVULSE.*

*Sonny picks up a TIFFANY LAMPSHADE—beautiful, stained glass—and places it gently on her head.*

**SONNY (CONT'D)**
Let there be light.

*She IGNITES. The flames reflect in his eyes.*

*He looks past her, out at the water.*

*There—anchored in the harbor—is a YACHT. Massive. Lit up like a casino.*

*On the deck: a BOWLING ALLEY.*

*KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER.*

---

## ACT THREE: THE FALL

---

### SCENE 18

**EXT. KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER - DECK - NIGHT**

*Sonny pulls himself aboard. The yacht sways gently. Music and laughter echo from inside.*

*The bowling alley stretches before him—ten lanes, gleaming under string lights. This is how Kewpie practices. The sway of the ship makes every shot harder.*

*GOONS emerge from the shadows, armed with BOWLING PINS, SHOES, BALLS, BEER BOTTLES, LIFE PRESERVERS.*

**SONNY**
*(cracking his neck)*
Alright. Let's roll.

---

### SCENE 19

**EXT. KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER - BOWLING ALLEY - CONTINUOUS**

*Sonny fights his way down the lanes. Pins FLY. Balls CRASH. It's poetry in violence.*

*He reaches the far end. A door leads below deck.*

*MECHANICAL ROOM.*

---

### SCENE 20

**INT. KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER - MECHANICAL ROOM - NIGHT**

*Pipes. Steam. The churning heart of the ship.*

*And there, waiting for him: KEWPIE.*

*He's exactly what you'd expect from the name—round, pink, cherubic, utterly grotesque. He wears a bowling shirt stretched to its limit and a smile that doesn't reach his eyes.*

**KEWPIE**
Sonny Tufts. The almost-champion.

**SONNY**
Where is she?

**KEWPIE**
*(laughing)*
Lucy? She's around. But I don't think she wants to see you right now. You've made quite the mess tonight.

**SONNY**
I'm just getting started.

*They circle each other. This isn't a brawl—this is a FIGHT. Technical. Brutal. Personal.*

*They trade blows. Kewpie is faster than he looks, stronger than he should be. But Sonny has rage on his side.*

*The fight goes on. Blood splatters across the machinery. Teeth fly. Bones crack.*

*Finally—Sonny gets the upper hand. He reaches into his bag and pulls out his secret weapon:*

*A BOWLING BALL LAUNCHER. Modified into an over-the-shoulder ROCKET LAUNCHER.*

*Kewpie's eyes go wide.*

**KEWPIE (CONT'D)**
Now wait just a—

*Sonny SHOVES the launcher somewhere bowling balls were never meant to go.*

*Flips the switch to AUTO.*

*Holds down the trigger.*

*Bowling balls FIRE into Kewpie. One after another. His body EXPANDS with each shot—grotesquely inflating like a water balloon.*

*Kewpie screams. His skin stretches. His eyes bulge.*

**KEWPIE (CONT'D)**
STOP! STOP! I'LL GIVE YOU ANYTHING—

*Sonny doesn't stop.*

*Kewpie swells to the size of an elephant.*

*And then—*

*POP.*

*The room is painted in crimson. Sonny stands in the center, covered head to toe in gore.*

*Silence.*

*Then—a SCREAM from above.*

*He looks up.*

*On a balcony overlooking the mechanical room: LUCY. She witnessed everything.*

*Tears stream down her face. Her hands cover her mouth.*

**LUCY**
You... you MONSTER.

*She turns and RUNS.*

---

### SCENE 21

**INT. KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER - BELOW DECK - NIGHT**

*Sonny chases her through the ship. Room after room. A labyrinth of excess and perversion—sadistic toys, masochistic furnishings, things that defy description.*

*But his steps grow slower. The adrenaline is fading. The weight of the night is catching up.*

*He passes through a room filled with masks. A room of mirrors. A room where the walls are padded and the floor is... sticky.*

*He doesn't look anymore. Just keeps moving.*

---

### SCENE 22

**EXT. KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER - STERN - DAWN**

*The first light of the new year breaks over the horizon.*

*Sonny pushes through the back door, expecting—hoping—to find her waiting for him.*

*The deck is empty.*

*He walks to the railing. Looks out at the water.*

*There—in the distance—a SINGLE-ENGINE FISHING BOAT putters away from the yacht.*

*At the helm: LUCY. Alone. Not looking back.*

*Her sobs carry across the water, fading with each passing moment.*

*Sonny stands there, blood-soaked and broken, watching her disappear into the dawn.*

*The sun rises. The water sparkles. Somewhere, a seagull cries.*

*He doesn't move.*

---

### SCENE 23

**EXT. OCEAN - WIDE SHOT - CONTINUOUS**

*The yacht sits still in the water. The fishing boat is a speck on the horizon.*

*Sonny remains at the railing—a silhouette against the morning light.*

*He finally got what he came for.*

*And lost everything in the process.*

---

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**TITLE CARD:**

*"She keeps running. You keep following."*

**THE END.**
