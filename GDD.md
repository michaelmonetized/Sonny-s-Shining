# SONNY'S SHINING
## Game Design Document v1.0

---

# PART ONE: CHARACTER BIBLE

> **Art Direction Note:** All characters adhere strictly to a **rubber hose / Fleischer Studios / Roger Rabbit** aesthetic. Limbs are noodle-like and boneless. Hands are four-fingered with white gloves (unless otherwise noted). Eyes are large, expressive pie-cuts or button eyes. Movement should feel springy, exaggerated, and perpetually in motion even when idle. All characters have subtle anthropomorphic animal traits blended seamlessly with their human designs—fur textures, ears, tails, snouts—rendered in the 1930s cartoon style. Black outlines. Limited but punchy color palettes. Think Max Fleischer meets noir.

---

## 1. SONNY (Hero)

**Inspiration:** Sonny Tufts  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Hound Dog

### Visual Description

Sonny is a lanky, athletic hound dog man with droopy yet determined eyes—the kind of sad-sack face that says "I've seen some things, but I'm still standing." His muzzle is long and expressive, with a permanently damp black nose that twitches when he's onto something.

**Build:** Tall and wiry with exaggerated long limbs perfect for the rubber hose style. His torso is barrel-shaped but lean—a bowler's physique. Arms stretch and snap with elastic energy when throwing.

**Costume:** 
- Cream-colored short-sleeve bowling shirt with burgundy piping and "SONNY" embroidered in cursive on the chest pocket
- High-waisted charcoal slacks held up by suspenders (one always slipping off the shoulder)
- Two-tone saddle shoes (cream and oxblood)
- His signature **ball-shining towel** hangs from his back pocket like a tail, faded burgundy with frayed edges

**Fur/Coloring:** Warm caramel base coat with cream underbelly and darker brown patches around his floppy ears. His ears bounce and flap with every movement.

**Face Details:**
- Heavy-lidded eyes that go from "lovesick" to "murder mode" in a single frame
- Thick black eyebrows that communicate everything
- A five o'clock shadow rendered as stippled crosshatching on his jowls
- One chipped canine tooth visible when he grins

**Idle Animation:** Sonny rolls a phantom bowling ball in his hands, occasionally bringing his towel up to polish it. His ears twitch. His nose sniffs the air. He's always looking for her.

## 2. LUCY (Princess)

**Inspiration:** Lucille Ball  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Red Fox

### Visual Description

Lucy is a knockout—a sleek red fox femme fatale with legs that go all the way up and a smile that goes all the way through you. She's drawn in the Betty Boop tradition but with vulpine sharpness: pointed ears that swivel toward sounds, a fluffy tail that swishes with her mood, and eyes that hold secrets.

**Build:** Hourglass figure with exaggerated hips and a tiny waist. Long, elegant limbs. She moves like smoke—sinuous, impossible to catch.

**Costume:**
- Floor-length black sequined evening gown with a scandalous thigh-high slit
- White elbow-length opera gloves
- A string of pearls that catch the moonlight
- Strappy silver heels that click-clack as she runs (always just out of reach)

**Fur/Coloring:** Rich burnt-orange fur with cream chest and muzzle. The tip of her tail is cream-colored, making it visible even in dark scenes as she disappears around corners.

**Face Details:**
- Huge doe eyes with heavy lashes (classic cartoon style)
- Beauty mark on her left cheek
- Red-orange curls that bounce like springs, styled in a 1940s wave
- A small, knowing smile that never quite reveals her intentions

**Signature Element:** Her **giggle**—visualized as floating musical notes and small hearts that trail behind her. The player will learn to hate and love this giggle.

**Animation Notes:** Lucy is rarely fully on screen. She's glimpsed in silhouettes, in reflections, disappearing through doorways. When visible, her tail always leads her exit, the last thing the player sees.

## 3. KEWPIE (Antagonist / Final Boss)

**Inspiration:** Kewpie Morgan  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Pig

### Visual Description

Kewpie is grotesque wealth made flesh—a corpulent pig man with the face of a cherub and the soul of a loan shark. He's designed to be simultaneously adorable and revolting, evoking the Kewpie doll aesthetic but corrupted, bloated, wrong.

**Build:** Spherical. Perfectly round torso balanced on tiny trotters. Short arms with stubby fingers weighted down by diamond pinky rings. His body jiggles with every laugh.

**Costume:**
- Custom-tailored white tuxedo with bowling-pin-shaped lapels
- Pink cummerbund stretched to its limit
- Patent leather shoes buffed to a mirror shine
- A bowling ball tie pin with a real diamond for the finger hole

**Skin/Coloring:** Baby pink skin, smooth and hairless, with a slight sheen like he's been glazed. Rosy cheeks. Curly tail that corkscrews when he's pleased.

**Face Details:**
- Tiny black button eyes that disappear into his fat cheeks when he smiles
- Porcine snout, always slightly upturned (literally looking down his nose at everyone)
- Cherubic lips in a permanent smirk
- Slicked-back hair so shiny it reflects light like a mirror

**Boss Form:** During the final fight, Kewpie's tuxedo tears away to reveal his bowling shirt underneath—the same style as Sonny's but in garish gold. He fights dirty, using every advantage.

**Death Animation:** As bowling balls fill him, his body inflates like a balloon—limbs stretching impossibly, eyes bulging, cheeks puffing. The rubber hose style is pushed to its horrifying extreme before the pop.

## 4. BERTIE (Level 1 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Bert Williams  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Bear  
**Location:** Bertie's Bustling Bubbles

### Visual Description

Bertie is an imposing grizzly bear man with the dignified bearing of a maître d' who's seen empires rise and fall from behind his bar. He's huge—a wall of muscle hidden beneath a bartender's professionalism—but there's something sad in his eyes that suggests he wasn't always in Kewpie's pocket.

**Build:** Massive. Shoulders like boulders. Arms thick as tree trunks. When he throws bottles, they're missiles. His apron barely contains his bulk.

**Costume:**
- Crisp white bartender's shirt with sleeve garters (gold)
- Black vest with subtle pinstripes
- Long white apron, perpetually spotless despite the chaos
- Bow tie (black, perfectly tied)

**Fur/Coloring:** Deep brown with silver-gray around the muzzle suggesting age and experience. His paws are lighter, almost tan.

**Face Details:**
- World-weary eyes with heavy bags
- A knowing, slow-spreading smile (genuinely menacing)
- Small round ears that flatten when he's angry
- One gold tooth that glints when he laughs

**Combat Style:** Bertie is a thrower. He juggles bottles, glasses, and cocktail shakers with bartender precision before hurling them. His attack patterns are rhythmic, almost musical.

**Fatality:** Crammed ass-first into a trash can, folded like an accordion, wheeled out to the alley. The final frame shows his little round ears poking out of the garbage.

## 5. CHARLIE (Level 2 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Charlie Chaplin  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Fire-Engine-Red Crab + Field Mouse  
**Location:** Charlie's Cracking Cantina

### Visual Description

Charlie is the game's most absurd creation—a **gigantic fire-engine-red crab** the size of a small car, with **luxurious Fabio-esque blonde hair** flowing from its shell. Nestled in this golden mane is the true boss: a tiny **field mouse** with a perfect Charlie Chaplin mustache, holding miniature reins connected to the crab's eyestalks.

#### THE CRAB (Claude)
**Build:** Wide, low to the ground, with eight scuttling legs and two massive claws capable of snipping through dumpsters. The shell is glossy and wet-looking.

**Coloring:** Candy-apple red with orange accents on the claw tips. The underbelly is pale cream.

**Details:**
- Googly eyes on stalks that swivel independently
- The blonde hair grows from a wig that's somehow fused to the shell
- Bubbles occasionally escape from its mouth when stressed
- Moves sideways with surprising speed

#### THE MOUSE (Charlie)
**Build:** Tiny. Could fit in Sonny's palm. Wears a miniature chef's toque.

**Coloring:** Gray-brown with cream belly. The mustache is jet black and perfectly rectangular.

**Details:**
- Holds tiny reins like a chariot driver
- Speaks in a squeaky voice that echoes strangely
- His hat is too big and keeps slipping over his eyes
- Visible sweat drops when panicking

**Combat Style:** The crab charges and snaps while the mouse throws kitchen implements with uncanny accuracy. They move as one—when the mouse pulls left, Claude goes left.

**Fatality:** Legs tied up like a hair scrunchie, crammed into a stock pot, lobbed over the fence into a garbage truck. The compactor engages. A trail of juices leaks from the departing truck.

## 6. DESI (Level 3 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Desi Arnaz  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Rooster  
**Location:** Desi's Darling Dwellings

### Visual Description

Desi is a vain, territorial rooster man who rules his crumbling apartment building with an iron claw. He's all puffed-up ego and Latin machismo, strutting around in his silk bathrobe like he's the king of a castle instead of a slumlord in a fire trap.

**Build:** Medium height but carries himself tall. Lean with a proud, puffed-out chest. Thin legs with scaled feet and prominent spurs.

**Costume:**
- Gaudy silk bathrobe in peacock blues and golds (monogrammed "D.D.D." for Desi's Darling Dwellings)
- Slippers with curled toes
- Nothing underneath (implied, not shown)
- A ridiculously ornate wire coat hanger clutched as a weapon

**Feathers/Coloring:** Iridescent black-green plumage on his body, brilliant red comb and wattle, golden-rust tail feathers that trail behind him dramatically.

**Face Details:**
- Sharp beak, always tilted upward
- Beady, judgmental eyes
- The comb flops when he's hit, enraging him further
- Thin mustache lines drawn along his beak

**Combat Style:** Desi orchestrates the tenants from above, crowing commands. He throws household items and swoops down to attack with his coat hanger. His crows are shockwaves.

**Fatality:** Coat hanger shoved down his throat, hung on a clothesline, sent zipping across the alley. The wire can't hold. He falls. *SPLAT.* His robe flutters down after him like a sad flag.

## 7. TIPPI (Level 4 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Tippi Hedren  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Raven  
**Location:** Tippi's Tantalizing Tango

### Visual Description

Tippi is a sleek, dangerous raven woman who runs the city's most exclusive "dance studio"—a front for activities that require leather, cages, and discretion. She's beautiful in a sharp, predatory way, all angles and gleaming black feathers.

**Build:** Tall and willowy with exaggerated long legs (the rubber hose style makes them almost serpentine). Her wings are incorporated into a feathered cape that spreads when she attacks.

**Costume:**
- Black leather corset with silver buckles
- Thigh-high stiletto boots (8+ inches, she walks on them like they're flats)
- Long black opera gloves with pointed metal tips on each finger
- A small black top hat perched between her pointed ears

**Feathers/Coloring:** Jet black with purple-blue iridescence. Her chest feathers form a natural V-neck.

**Face Details:**
- Sharp yellow beak, slightly hooked
- Heavily lined eyes with dramatic winged eyeliner (meta-joke)
- Piercing yellow-green eyes that never blink
- A beauty mark mole below her left eye

**Combat Style:** Tippi is fast and vicious. She throws stilettos like daggers, cracks batons, and uses her customers as weapons. Her attacks have a dance-like rhythm.

**Fatality:** Impaled on a dance pole from tooth to tailbone. Launched like a javelin through the window. She spins as she falls—one final grotesque dance.

## 8. BESSIE (Level 5 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Bessie Love  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Cat  
**Location:** Bessie's Bouncing Ballroom

### Visual Description

Bessie is a hip, slick jazz cat—literally. A sleek feline DJ who controls the dancefloor like a puppet master. She's small but commands enormous presence, her every movement synchronized to the beat.

**Build:** Petite and compact with a dancer's grace. Her tail acts as a third limb, snatching records, gesturing commands, maintaining her balance.

**Costume:**
- Oversized vintage headphones clamped over her tall ears
- Sequined flapper dress in deep purple
- Fingerless gloves for better grip on vinyl
- Platform boots that add four inches to her height

**Fur/Coloring:** Solid black with a white chest patch shaped like a bow tie. Her eyes glow in the club's darkness.

**Face Details:**
- Large, luminous golden eyes with vertical slit pupils
- Whiskers that vibrate to the bass
- Small pink nose that twitches with satisfaction
- Theatrical makeup—dark lips, exaggerated lashes

**Combat Style:** Bessie slings vinyl records like deadly frisbees. Each record has a different flight pattern based on its genre (jazz curves, rock flies straight, disco spirals). She also commands bouncers with hand signals.

**The Key:** Bessie dramatically swallows the front door key at the start of the fight. It's visible as a lump traveling down her throat. The player must defeat her to escape.

**Fatality:** A vinyl record shoved into her mouth, slicing her cheeks into a Chelsea smile. Then—gripped like a bowling ball (eye sockets, belly button)—she's hurled through the glass doors into traffic.

---

## 9. HARRY (Level 6 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Harry "Snub" Pollard  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Bulldog  
**Location:** Harry's Holy Haulers

### Visual Description

Harry is a squat, muscular bulldog man with grease-stained overalls and a handlebar mustache that would be comical if he wasn't so clearly a killer. He runs a chop shop where the "parts" aren't always automotive.

**Build:** Low and wide. Barrel chest. Stubby bowed legs with massive thighs. His arms are too long for his body, perfect for swinging wrenches.

**Costume:**
- Greasy navy mechanic's coveralls with "HARRY" on a patch
- Steel-toed boots covered in mysterious stains
- A trucker cap worn backward
- Tool belt jangling with weapons

**Fur/Coloring:** Brindle pattern—tan and dark brown in uneven patches. His jowls are loose and flap when he talks. His underbite reveals gold-capped teeth filed to points.

**Face Details:**
- Pushed-in face with a perpetually wet nose
- Small, mean eyes too close together
- The famous mustache: waxed, curled, and magnificent
- A scar running across his muzzle

**Combat Style:** Harry throws heavy mechanical objects—mufflers, engine blocks, socket wrenches. His mechanics swarm with pipe wrenches and jack handles. The fight is loud and industrial.

**Fatality:** Tow hook through the chest, out the back. The winch activates. Harry spins, wrapped in cable, tighter and tighter. One final yank slices him into perfect discs like deli meat.

## 10. IVY (Level 7 Boss)

**Inspiration:** Ivy Lee  
**Anthropomorphic Base:** Owl  
**Location:** Ivy's Illuminating Imports

### Visual Description

Ivy is an elegant great horned owl woman who runs the city's most exclusive import operation from her dock-side tower. She's old money and older secrets, draped in pearls and surrounded by priceless antiques she uses as weapons.

**Build:** Tall and slim with perfect posture. Her wings fold into an elegant cape. She carries herself with aristocratic disdain.

**Costume:**
- Long pearl-gray evening gown with feathered trim
- Multiple strands of pearls (some are real, some are weapons)
- Elbow-length white gloves
- A small pillbox hat with a tiny veil

**Feathers/Coloring:** Mottled brown and cream in classic owl patterns. Her facial disc is heart-shaped and pale, making her yellow eyes even more striking.

**Face Details:**
- Enormous yellow eyes that never blink and glow in darkness
- Small hooked beak, gray
- Prominent ear tufts that stand up straight when agitated
- Expression of eternal disapproval

**Combat Style:** Ivy hurls antique lamps from her crow's nest tower. Each lamp is lovingly detailed—Tiffany, Handel, Pairpoint—and she grieves each one she throws. The player must climb the tower while dodging.

**Fatality:** A Tiffany lamp shoved down her throat. The cord ripped out through her gut. Plugged into an outlet. A lampshade placed on her head. She lights up, smokes, and collapses.

## ENSEMBLE CHARACTERS

### Waitresses (Level 1)
Black and white cat-girls in classic maid outfits, wielding brooms. Move in synchronized patterns.

### Kitchen Staff (Level 2)
Various rodents—rats and mice—in chef whites. Throw kitchen implements with surprising accuracy.

### Tenants (Level 3)
A menagerie of apartment-dwelling creatures: cats, pigeons, raccoons. All in nightwear, all armed with household objects, all very angry about the noise.

### Club Patrons/Customers (Levels 4-5)
Shadowy figures in leather and lace. Mostly silhouettes with glowing eyes. Species ambiguous, which is somehow worse.

### Bouncers (Level 5)
Massive gorilla men in black suits. They don't throw—they punch, grab, and crush.

### Mechanics (Level 6)
Greasy hyenas with cackling attacks. They travel in packs and coordinate assaults.

### Dock Workers (Level 7)
Burly walruses and seals in longshoreman gear. Slow but devastating when they connect.

### Ship Crew (Level 8)
Penguins in sailor suits. Surprisingly vicious. Armed with bowling equipment.

# PART TWO: LEVEL LOCATIONS

> **Camera Note for Animators:** Main gameplay uses a **Diner Dash isometric angle**—3/4 top-down view allowing players to see maze layouts, enemy patterns, and projectile trajectories. Boss fights transition to **Street Fighter/Mortal Kombat side-view** for one-on-one combat. The bonus chase level uses **true overhead/top-down** like classic racing games. Interstitial cuts begin **first-person** then **zoom out** to establish new location.

---

## LEVEL 1: BERTIE'S BUSTLING BUBBLES

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** The screen fills with champagne bubbles. Confetti drifts. The sounds of celebration—jazz, laughter, clinking glasses—wash over the player.

**ZOOM OUT:** We pull back from Sonny's POV to reveal the bar in all its glory. Art deco elegance with a speakeasy soul. The walls are deep burgundy velvet. Brass fixtures gleam. A long mahogany bar dominates the left side, backed by a mirror and glittering bottles. 

**THE SPACE:** Circular cocktail tables dot the checkered floor in a maze-like arrangement. A small stage with a jazz trio. A dance floor packed with celebrating couples. Chandeliers drip crystals. Cigarette smoke curls toward the tin ceiling.

**LIGHTING:** Warm amber from the chandeliers, punctuated by the cold blue of a "HAPPY 1935" sign flickering in the window.

**THE CLOCK:** Prominently displayed—11:47 PM. It advances in real-time during the opening cutscene.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:** 
- Spilled drinks (slippery patches)
- Broken glass (damage zones)
- Crowd dancers (moving obstacles)
- The bar itself (cover, but Bertie throws over it)

---

## LEVEL 2: CHARLIE'S CRACKING CANTINA (The Alley)

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** The back door of the bar slams shut behind us. Cold air. The smell of garbage. A single bare bulb flickers above.

**ZOOM OUT:** A narrow alley between brick buildings, choked with refuse. Dumpsters overflow. Fire escapes zigzag overhead. Steam vents create rolling fog. At the far end, silhouetted against a streetlight: Lucy's unmistakable shape disappearing through a door.

**THE SPACE:** The alley is a gauntlet. Dumpsters, trash cans, stacked boxes, and debris form the maze. Puddles reflect the moonlight. Graffiti covers the walls in period-appropriate styles.

**LIGHTING:** Harsh contrasts—pools of light from the occasional bulb, deep shadows everywhere else. The full moon is visible in the sliver of sky above.

**KEY VISUAL:** The garbage truck at the far end, its compactor grinding ominously, headlights cutting through the fog like predator eyes.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Slippery puddles
- Unstable box stacks (can collapse on player OR enemies)
- Steam vents (obscure vision, cause minor damage)
- The garbage truck compactor (instant death zone, used in fatality)

---

## LEVEL 3: DESI'S DARLING DWELLINGS (The Fire Escape)

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** We look up. Way up. The building towers above us, a crooked stack of misery and architectural violations. A woman's silhouette is visible against the full moon on the rooftop—Lucy, laughing, carefree.

**ZOOM OUT:** A vertical slice of urban decay. The building is brownstone and brick, five stories of narrow windows and rusting fire escapes. Every window is lit, every tenant angry. The fire escape zigzags up the face like a scar.

**THE SPACE:** This level is primarily VERTICAL. The fire escape platforms form the "maze"—each landing a combat arena. Ladders connect them. The ground gets further away with each climb.

**LIGHTING:** Windows cast rectangles of warm light onto the cold metal of the fire escape. The moon looms overhead. Lightning occasionally flashes in the distance (purely atmospheric).

**KEY VISUAL:** Desi at the top, silhouetted in his garish robe, his comb and tail feathers visible against the sky. He's directing traffic like a conductor of chaos.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Falling objects (constant rain from above)
- Loose platforms (wobble, threaten to collapse)
- Clotheslines (can entangle OR be used as ziplines)
- The drop (falling damages, falling too far kills)

---

## LEVEL 4: TIPPI'S TANTALIZING TANGO

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** Wind. Speed. The power line rushes beneath us. The window ahead grows larger, larger—

*CRASH*

Glass explodes. We tumble through, landing in—

**ZOOM OUT:** A room that shouldn't exist in polite society. Red walls. Black leather. Chrome poles catching the light. Cages suspended from the ceiling cast barred shadows across everything. Massage tables line the walls, their restraints dangling. A disco ball rotates slowly, scattering light like a nervous heartbeat.

**THE SPACE:** The "dance studio" is a maze of perversion—cages create corridors, poles block paths, leather furniture provides cover that might grab back. The elevator is visible at the far end, golden and inviting.

**LIGHTING:** Dim red with pulsing accent lights. Shadows are deep and move. The disco ball provides the only "clean" light, and it's unsettling.

**KEY VISUAL:** Tippi on her central stage, posed like a showgirl, surrounded by her "students." The dance pole behind her gleams with lethal promise.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Cages (enemies can lock player inside temporarily)
- Poles (block movement, can be used for swinging attacks)
- Suspended chains (swing across, or get hit by them)
- "Equipment" (various... objects... that serve as projectiles)

## LEVEL 5: BESSIE'S BOUNCING BALLROOM

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** The elevator plummets. Our stomach drops. The crank spins uselessly. Then—BOOM. Dust. Silence. We step out—

**ZOOM OUT:** A cathedral of noise and bodies, now frozen. The nightclub is enormous—a converted warehouse with towering ceilings. The dance floor is a sea of faces, all turned toward us. The DJ booth rises at the far end like an altar, Bessie behind her turntables, records stacked like fortifications.

**THE SPACE:** The club floor is a maze of cocktail tables, velvet ropes, support pillars, and frozen dancers. Bouncers are positioned throughout. The exit—glass double doors with an Art Deco sunrise—is locked behind Bessie.

**LIGHTING:** Strobes. Colored gels. Spinning spots. The lighting actively changes throughout the level, alternating between too-bright flashes and near-darkness.

**KEY VISUAL:** Bessie at her booth, headphones clamped, eyes glowing, a record in each hand ready to throw. The key-swallowing animation plays prominently.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Strobe blindness (periodic vision impairment)
- Crowd surge (groups of dancers become moving obstacles)
- Velvet ropes (trip hazards, can be weaponized)
- The sound system (speakers can be knocked over for area damage)

## BONUS LEVEL: THE CHASE

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** We burst through the shattered doors. Traffic. Chaos. A taxi screeches up where the limo just was. The driver doesn't know what's coming.

**TRANSITION TO OVERHEAD:** The camera rockets upward to a true top-down view. The city streets become a maze of moving cars, pedestrians, and obstacles.

**THE SPACE:** The city at night, rendered as a scrolling overhead maze. The bowling-pin limo is always just ahead, weaving through traffic. The taxi follows, controlled by the player.

**LIGHTING:** Neon signs. Streetlights. Headlights. The city is a river of light and shadow.

**KEY VISUAL:** The limo's ridiculous silhouette, always just out of reach. Cars swerving. Pedestrians diving. Fire hydrants exploding as we clip them.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Oncoming traffic (instant crash)
- Pedestrians (slows you down, moral implications)
- Intersections (cross-traffic ambush)
- THE TOW TRUCK (level-ending T-bone crash)

## LEVEL 6: HARRY'S HOLY HAULERS

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** Pain. Ringing ears. We crawl from twisted metal. Through smoke and sparks, we see figures approaching with weapons raised—

**ZOOM OUT:** An airplane hangar, repurposed for darker work. Gutted vehicles in various stages of dismemberment fill the space. Engine hoists, tire stacks, and tool racks create the maze. The concrete floor is stained with oil and other fluids. The tow truck that hit us is visible through the hole in the wall.

**THE SPACE:** Industrial horror. The chop shop is a labyrinth of car corpses—doors, hoods, trunks, all arranged to funnel the player through kill zones. The winch crane looms at the far end, Harry's platform.

**LIGHTING:** Harsh work lights on cables, swinging slightly. Deep shadows between vehicle stacks. Sparks flying from somewhere. The back door is visible as a rectangle of moonlight.

**KEY VISUAL:** Harry on his platform, silhouetted against a bank of tool racks, the tow hook swinging on its cable like a pendulum. A grim clock.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Oil slicks (slip, fire hazard)
- Suspended engines (can be dropped on enemies or player)
- Active car lifts (crush zones)
- Sparking exposed wires (damage zones)
- Gasoline drums (explode when hit)

## LEVEL 7: IVY'S ILLUMINATING IMPORTS

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** We swing on the cable, the hangar shrinking behind us. Below—water. Containers. The smell of salt and diesel. We land, roll, stand—

**ZOOM OUT:** A wharf at night. Shipping containers stacked like children's blocks create a maze of steel canyons. Between them: luxury cars, gleaming and incongruous. Streetlights are sparse and struggling. The tower rises at the end—a crow's nest of welded containers, Ivy's silhouette visible at the summit.

**THE SPACE:** The dock is vast and dark. Containers form corridors that dead-end, loop back, ambush. The cars provide cover but also blind spots. The tower climb is the level's climax—a vertical gauntlet.

**LIGHTING:** Near-total darkness punctuated by struggling streetlights. The water reflects moonlight. The tower's top has a warm glow—Ivy's antique lamps.

**KEY VISUAL:** The lamps. Throughout the level, you see them silhouetted against the sky as Ivy hurls them—beautiful, expensive, deadly. And beyond the tower, visible throughout: Kewpie's yacht, lit up like a casino, bowling lanes gleaming on its deck.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Darkness (limited visibility, enemies hidden)
- Container gaps (can fall through)
- The tower climb (falling damage, constant projectiles)
- Electrified antiques (after defeating Ivy)

---

## LEVEL 8: KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER

### Opening Frame Description

**FIRST PERSON VIEW:** We pull ourselves over the railing, dripping wet. The deck spreads before us. Music. Laughter. And then—silence. Every head turns.

**ZOOM OUT:** A floating palace of excess. The yacht is Art Deco opulence gone cancerous—gold fixtures, velvet ropes, champagne fountains. And running down the center of the deck: ten full bowling lanes, gleaming under string lights. Pin-setters clunk rhythmically. The ship sways, making every throw a calculation.

**THE SPACE:** The bowling alley IS the maze. Lanes provide channels, gutters are trenches, pin racks are cover. Below deck: the engine room, Kewpie's arena.

**LIGHTING:** Party lights—strings of bulbs, a disco ball, the glow from the city in the distance. Below deck: harsh industrial emergency lighting.

**KEY VISUAL:** The lanes stretching toward the cabin. Kewpie's private door at the end, flanked by goons. Above it: a window. Behind that window, barely visible: red hair.

**ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS:**
- Ship sway (affects movement, aim, and enemy attacks)
- Bowling balls (rolling hazards AND weapons)
- Lanes (slippery, hard to change direction)
- Champagne fountains (visual obstruction)

## THE LABYRINTH (Post-Boss Chase)

### Description

After defeating Kewpie, Sonny chases Lucy through the yacht's interior—a surreal maze of excess and deviance. This is not a combat section; it's an exploration/environmental storytelling sequence.

**ROOMS INCLUDE:**
- The Mirror Room (infinite reflections of Sonny's blood-soaked form)
- The Mask Room (walls of faces, watching)
- The Red Room (everything red—furniture, walls, implications)
- The Collection (trophies from Kewpie's "wins"—bowling and otherwise)

**PURPOSE:** Slow the pace before the ending. Let the player see what they fought through the city for. Make them question if it was worth it.

# PART THREE: WORLD MAP

### The Map Screen

The world map is rendered as a **noir city at night**—a stylized overhead view of the urban sprawl, black buildings against a deep purple sky. The route from level to level is marked by a **dotted line** that traces Sonny's journey through the city.

**VISUAL STYLE:** 
- Black silhouette buildings with occasional lit windows
- Neon signs mark each location (pulsing when active)
- The route snakes through the city, showing the chaos of the chase

# PART FOUR: GAME MECHANICS

## 4.1 CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

### Main Levels (Diner Dash Perspective)

**OBJECTIVE:** Navigate maze-like environments from entry point to boss arena while defeating/avoiding enemies.

**PLAYER CONTROLS:**
- **Movement:** 8-directional (WASD / Left Stick)
- **Primary Attack:** Melee strike (punch/kick combo system)
- **Secondary Attack:** Towel catch-and-return (hold for catch window, release to throw)
- **Dodge:** Quick dash with invincibility frames
- **Interact:** Context-sensitive (pick up objects, use environment)
- **Special:** Bowling Ball Launcher (when unlocked/charged)

**MOVEMENT MECHANICS:**
- Standard walk speed
- Sprint (drains stamina, breaks after 3 seconds)
- Dodge roll (i-frames, short cooldown)
- Environmental interaction (vault over tables, slide under obstacles)

**COMBAT MECHANICS:**
- Light attack (fast, low damage)
- Heavy attack (slow, high damage, breaks guards)
- Grab (close range, leads to throw or combo)
- Counter (perfect timing parry, triggers slow-mo return)

**THE TOWEL SYSTEM:**
Core unique mechanic. Sonny's ball-shining towel can:
1. **Catch** incoming projectiles (timing window system—perfect, good, miss)
2. **Return** caught projectiles at enemies (aim with right stick/mouse)
3. **Whip** close-range as secondary melee (stuns enemies)
4. **Grapple** environmental objects (ziplines, swinging)

**Perfect Catch:** Slow-mo. Extra damage on return. Satisfying sound effect.
**Good Catch:** Normal return. Standard damage.
**Miss:** Take full projectile damage. 

---

### Boss Fights (Fighting Game Perspective)

**CAMERA:** Side-view, Street Fighter style. Background is the level's environment in 2D.

**HEALTH BARS:** 
- Player: Green bar, three segments
- Boss: Red bar, multiple segments based on difficulty

**COMBAT CHANGES:**
- Dedicated block button/direction
- Combo system with juggle potential
- Boss-specific attack patterns to learn
- Environmental hazards visible in background

**FATALITY SYSTEM:**
When boss health reaches zero, "FINISH HIM" prompt appears. Player executes button sequence to trigger brutal fatality cutscene. Missing the window results in a less spectacular (but still fatal) finisher.

---

### Bonus Chase Level (Overhead Perspective)

**CAMERA:** True top-down, scrolling vertically

**CONTROLS:**
- **Accelerate:** Automatic (speed increases over time)
- **Steer:** Left/Right only
- **Brake:** Slows slightly (limited use)
- **Ram:** Short boost with damage hitbox

**MECHANICS:**
- Avoid oncoming traffic (instant crash ends segment)
- Stay on road (going off-road slows and damages)
- Collect power-ups (speed, shields, weapons)
- Chase the limo (keep it on screen to progress)

**LEVEL STRUCTURE:**
- Timed segments
- Checkpoints at major intersections
- The T-bone crash is scripted—unavoidable story beat

## 4.4 CAMERA SYSTEMS

### Isometric Main View (Diner Dash Style)

**SCROLL:** Camera leads player slightly in movement direction
**BOUNDARIES:** Soft lock; camera won't show beyond playable area

**CAMERA TRIGGERS:**
- Boss rooms: Lock camera to arena
- Scripted sequences: Cinematic rails
- Chase sequences: Accelerated scroll

---

### Fighting Game Boss View

**ANGLE:** True side view (0-degree)
**PERSPECTIVE:** Slight perspective for depth
**ZOOM:** Dynamic; pulls out for jumping, pushes in for grabs
**BOUNDARIES:** Stage edges visible; fighters bounce off walls

**TRANSITIONS:**
- From Isometric: Dramatic pan and rotation (2 seconds)
- To Fatality: Camera shifts to optimal angle for violence

---

### Overhead Chase View

**ANGLE:** True top-down (90-degree)
**PERSPECTIVE:** Orthographic
**ZOOM:** Fixed; shows road width plus margins
**SCROLL:** Continuous upward; speed increases over time
