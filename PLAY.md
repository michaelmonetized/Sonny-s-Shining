# SONNY'S SHINING

### A Tragedy in Three Acts

_A love letter to classic beat-em-ups and the golden age of cinema's strangest stars._

---

## DRAMATIS PERSONAE

**SONNY** — A professional bowler. Tournament hopeful. Romantic fool.

**LUCY** — A bowling groupie. Her heart follows the leaderboard.

**KEWPIE** — Crime boss. Sonny's arch nemesis on the lanes and in love.

**BERTIE** — Bartender at Bertie's Bustling Bubbles.

**CHARLIE** — A grotesque chef. A crab with Fabio hair, puppeteered by a mouse.

**DESI** — A landlord. Lord of his darling dwellings.

**TIPPI** — Dance instructor. Runs a promiscuous establishment.

**BESSIE** — Deejay and club owner. Spinner of vinyl, swallower of keys.

**HARRY** — Tow truck operator. Runs a chop shop.

**IVY** — Importer of antiques. Keeper of the docks.

**ENSEMBLE** — Waitresses, Kitchen Staff, Tenants, Club Patrons, Mechanics, Dock Workers, Ship Crew

---

## SETTING

New Year's Eve. A rain-slicked city that moves like a film reel — all shadow and silver, all jazz and menace. The production should evoke silent film aesthetics: stark lighting, exaggerated gesture, title cards between scenes, and a live pianist underscoring the action.

Violence is stylized — shadow puppetry, slow-motion choreography, Foley artists visible onstage. The brutality should feel like a fever dream, operatic and absurd.

---

# ACT ONE

## Scene 1: The Bubbles

_BERTIE'S BUSTLING BUBBLES. New Year's Eve. Confetti drifts. Champagne flows. A clock reads 11:47 PM._

_SONNY and LUCY sit at a small table, their world a spotlight in the dim. She laughs at something he's said. His bowling bag rests at his feet like a loyal dog._

**SONNY:**  
And that's when the pins said to the ball — "We'll see you in the gutter."

_LUCY laughs — too loud, too bright, the laugh of someone performing joy._

**LUCY:**  
Oh, Sonny. You're going to win that tournament. I can feel it.

**SONNY:**  
With you in my corner? Sweetheart, I could bowl a perfect game blindfolded.

_He reaches for her hand. She lets him take it._

_BERTIE approaches, polishing a glass. He leans down, whispers in LUCY's ear. Her expression shifts — surprise, then something else. Hunger? Fear? She withdraws her hand._

**LUCY:**  
I have to powder my nose.

**SONNY:**  
Now? It's almost midnight—

**LUCY:**  
_(rising)_  
A lady's prerogative.

_She moves toward the bar. Past it. Through a door marked "PRIVATE."_

_SONNY half-rises, confused. Through the door's small window, he sees: KEWPIE's bowling-pin limousine. LUCY climbing inside. Her giggle echoing like a taunt._

**SONNY:**  
Lucy—!

_BERTIE blocks his path, grinning._

**BERTIE:**  
Where you going, pin-jockey?

**SONNY:**  
Get out of my way.

**BERTIE:**  
_(laughing, selecting a bottle)_  
She's already gone, friend. Already gone.

_BERTIE hurls a bottle. SONNY ducks. Glass shatters._

_The clock strikes 11:50. The WAITRESSES rise from the shadows, brooms raised like weapons._

_Title card descends: "AND SO IT BEGINS."_

_Chaos. Choreographed violence. SONNY uses his ball-shining towel to catch and return projectiles. He weaves through a maze of tables as the ENSEMBLE attacks._

_Finally, only BERTIE remains, atop the bar, laughing, hurling everything within reach._

**SONNY:**  
_(dodging)_  
You think this is funny?

**BERTIE:**  
Kewpie's gonna make her a queen, lane-rat. What were you gonna give her? A cheap trophy and a kiss?

_SONNY catches a thrown bottle. Hurls it back. It connects._

_BERTIE stumbles. SONNY advances._

**BERTIE:**  
_(desperate)_  
Wait—wait—

_The lights shift to shadow puppetry. We see BERTIE's silhouette: stuffed into a trash can. SONNY jumping on his belly. Folding him. Dragging the can to the alley._

_Sound: A lid clanging shut._

_Title card: "TAKING OUT THE TRASH."_

_BLACKOUT._

---

## Scene 2: The Alley

_An alley behind the bar. Steam rises from grates. Trash cans line the walls._

_SONNY emerges, dragging the can. He drops it. Catches his breath._

_At the far end of the alley: a silhouette. LUCY. She darts through a door on the right, her giggle fading like wind chimes._

**SONNY:**  
Lucy!

_A door bursts open. CHARLIE emerges — a grotesque creation: an actor in a massive crab costume, fire-engine red, with flowing blonde locks. On the crab's head: a tiny puppet of a mouse with a little toothbrush mustache, clearly "controlling" the crab below._

**CHARLIE (MOUSE):**  
_(high-pitched)_  
Nobody chases the boss's girl!

**CHARLIE (CRAB):**  
_(booming, dumb)_  
YEAH. WHAT HE SAID.

_CHARLIE begins hurling kitchen refuse. The KITCHEN STAFF flood from the door with boxes, pans, cleavers._

_Title card: "CHARLIE'S CRACKING CANTINA."_

_Another choreographed battle through a maze of dumpsters and boxes. SONNY fights his way toward CHARLIE._

**SONNY:**  
I don't want trouble. I just want to find her.

**CHARLIE (MOUSE):**  
Trouble found you, pin-pusher!

_The fight culminates. SONNY triumphs._

_Shadow puppetry again: The crab's legs tied together. The body stuffed into a stock pot. Lobbed over a fence. A garbage truck's hydraulics whine. Something crunches._

_SONNY stands alone. Looks up._

_Title card: "HE HEARD HER VOICE LIKE AN ANGEL."_

---

## Scene 3: The Fire Escape

_Above, silhouetted against a full moon projection: LUCY on a rooftop, chatting with an unseen figure. Carefree. Laughing._

**SONNY:**  
_(calling up)_  
LUCY!

_A window slams open. DESI leans out, furious._

**DESI:**  
KEEP IT QUIET DOWN THERE!

_He hurls a vase. It explodes beside SONNY._

**DESI:**  
This is a respectable building! Desi's Darling Dwellings don't tolerate riffraff!

_TENANTS appear in windows, armed with household objects: lamps, radios, air conditioning units._

_Title card: "THE ASCENT."_

_The set transforms: a vertical climbing structure representing the fire escape. SONNY climbs as objects rain down. Stylized combat on platforms._

_At the top: DESI, guarding the ladder to the roof._

**DESI:**  
Turn around, gutter-ball. She doesn't want you.

**SONNY:**  
Let me ask her myself.

**DESI:**  
The lady has made her choice. Some men are champagne. You're flat beer.

_They fight. DESI loses._

_Shadow puppetry: A coat hanger. Down the throat. Hung on a clothesline. Zipping across the alley._

_Sound: Distant impact._

_Title card: "HE DIDN'T MAKE IT."_

_SONNY climbs to the roof._

_It's deserted. Dark. Wet. The party's over. Nobody left._

_Then — faint — her giggle. Below. Club music thumping._

_SONNY runs to the roof's edge. Looks down._

_LUCY climbs into KEWPIE's bowling-pin limo. It speeds away, taking two right turns, disappearing behind a booming nightclub._

**SONNY:**  
_(quiet)_  
Why do you keep running?

_He pulls out his ball-shining towel. Looks at a power line stretching across to the next building._

_Title card: "THERE WAS ONLY ONE WAY DOWN."_

_He jumps._

_BLACKOUT._

---

# ACT TWO

## Scene 4: The Tango

_TIPPI'S TANTALIZING TANGO. A dance studio, but wrong. Cages. Leather. Dim red lighting. The aftermath of "after-hours activities."_

_CRASH. SONNY comes through a "window" (shattered glass sound, actors scatter). He rolls, rises, surveys the scene._

_TIPPI stands center stage in elaborate fetish-wear, outraged._

**TIPPI:**  
Do you have ANY idea what you've interrupted?

**SONNY:**  
I'm looking for a girl.

**TIPPI:**  
_(laughing)_  
Aren't they all? And they always come here. Eventually.

_The "CUSTOMERS" rise from massage tables and cages, armed with stilettos, batons, and... other implements._

**TIPPI:**  
Kewpie said you might come sniffing. Said to keep you... entertained.

_Title card: "THE SCHOOL OF HARD KNOCKS."_

_Battle through the maze of perversion. The choreography here is different — more sensual, more dangerous, a tango of violence._

_SONNY reaches the elevator. TIPPI blocks it._

**TIPPI:**  
She doesn't love you. She loves _winning_. And right now, Kewpie's got the high score.

**SONNY:**  
Then I'll just have to beat it.

_They fight. TIPPI loses._

_Shadow puppetry: A dance pole. The skewering. The launching. A distant scream._

_Title card: "EXIT, PURSUED BY GRAVITY."_

_SONNY enters the elevator. Pulls the crank. Freefall sound. Boom._

---

## Scene 5: The Ballroom

_BESSIE'S BOUNCING BALLROOM. The elevator doors open. Dust billows. The thumping club music goes silent._

_Everyone freezes. Turns._

_SONNY emerges from the fog, scanning the crowd. His eyes land on — LUCY._

_For a moment, time stops. They stare at each other._

**SONNY:**  
_(breathless)_  
Lucy.

_She scoffs. Actually scoffs. A sound of pure dismissal._

_LUCY turns and walks out the front door._

_BOUNCERS begin closing in. At the DJ booth, BESSIE drags her vinyl crates to the front door, locks it, swallows the key with theatrical exaggeration._

**BESSIE:**  
_(into microphone)_  
Ladies and gentlemen... tonight's entertainment... is PERSONAL.

_She begins slinging records like deadly frisbees._

_Title card: "SPIN CYCLE."_

_Battle through cocktail tables and velvet ropes. The BOUNCERS are massive, the records are razor-sharp, and somewhere outside, LUCY is getting away._

_SONNY reaches BESSIE._

**BESSIE:**  
You're pathetic, you know that? Chasing a woman who's running as fast as she can. Ever wonder why she runs, Sonny?

**SONNY:**  
She's confused. Kewpie's got her turned around.

**BESSIE:**  
_(laughing)_  
Oh, honey. Nobody's got that girl turned anywhere she didn't want to go.

_They fight. BESSIE loses._

_The violence here is the most extreme — the shadow puppetry shows the vinyl record, the widening smile, the bowling-ball grip, the launch through glass._

_Sound: Shattering. Horns. Screech._

_SONNY stands at the broken front door. Outside: LUCY, once again slinking into Kewpie's limo. It drives away._

_Title card: "SHE KEEPS RUNNING. HE KEEPS FOLLOWING."_

---

## Scene 6: The Chase

_A projection screen descends. An old-school video game aesthetic: a car dodging oncoming traffic._

_SONNY mimes driving, stealing a cab._

**SONNY:**  
Come on, come on, COME ON—

_The game plays out — he's gaining on the limo — closer — closer—_

_CRASH._

_A tow truck T-bones him. The projection freezes, shatters._

_Title card: "HARRY'S HOLY HAULERS."_

---

## Scene 7: The Hangar

_An airplane hangar. Or rather, a chop shop. Gutted vehicles everywhere. MECHANICS with wrenches and pipes._

_SONNY crawls from wreckage, bleeding._

**HARRY:**  
_(stepping forward, tow hook swinging)_  
Wrong place, wrong time, bowling boy. Kewpie's got eyes everywhere. And we got... tools.

_Title card: "DISASSEMBLY REQUIRED."_

_Battle through the automotive graveyard. Pipe wrenches swing. Jack handles slash. Mufflers fly._

_SONNY reaches HARRY._

**HARRY:**  
You're running on fumes, lane-jockey. And the tank's about empty.

**SONNY:**  
Still got enough for you.

_They fight. HARRY loses._

_Shadow puppetry: The tow hook. Through the chest. The spinning. The cable tightening. The slicing._

_Sound: Something wet hitting the floor. Multiple times._

_SONNY grabs a winch cable. Swings out the hangar's back door._

_Title card: "ONTO THE DOCKS."_

---

## Scene 8: The Docks

_IVY'S ILLUMINATING IMPORTS. A maze of shipping containers. Darkness. The occasional streetlight barely reveals the path._

_SONNY lands, releases the cable. Moves through shadows._

_Title card: "IN DARKNESS, DANGER."_

_DOCK WORKERS attack from the shadows. SONNY fights through containers and fancy cars._

_He reaches a tower — a crow's nest structure. Begins to climb._

_Above, IVY hurls antique lamps._

**IVY:**  
Do you have ANY idea how much these are worth?!

**SONNY:**  
_(climbing, dodging)_  
More than you'll be worth when I'm done!

_He reaches her._

**IVY:**  
Kewpie's ship has already sailed, fool. You'll never catch him now.

**SONNY:**  
Watch me.

_They fight. IVY loses._

_Shadow puppetry: The Tiffany lamp. Down the throat. The cord ripped out. The outlet. The lampshade._

_Sound: Buzzing. Flickering. A scream that becomes a sizzle._

_Title card: "LIT."_

_SONNY looks out at the harbor. A ship in the distance: KEWPIE'S KICKING KLIPPER._

_BLACKOUT._

---

# ACT THREE

## Scene 9: The Klipper

_The deck of a pleasure cruise. Debauched. Luxurious. And, improbably, containing an entire bowling alley._

_SONNY pulls himself aboard, dripping wet._

_CREW and PARTYGOERS turn._

**PARTYGOER:**  
Is that—?

**CREW MEMBER:**  
The bowler. He actually made it.

_They attack with everything: pins, shoes, balls, beer bottles, life preservers._

_Title card: "A PERFECT 300."_

_SONNY fights his way down the lanes. Using pins as weapons. Rolling balls into shins. Returning projectiles with his towel. This is his element. This is his game._

_He reaches a door: ENGINE ROOM._

_Kicks it open._

---

## Scene 10: The Showdown

_The engine room. Pipes and valves. Industrial hellscape._

_KEWPIE stands center stage. He's been waiting. He's... magnificent. Grotesque. A showman through and through._

**KEWPIE:**  
Sonny. My favorite lane-mate. You've made quite the mess tonight.

**SONNY:**  
Give her back.

**KEWPIE:**  
_(laughing)_  
Give? GIVE? Oh, Sonny. I didn't take her. She _chose_. That's what you never understood. That's why you'll never beat me — on the lanes or in love. You think it's about skill. It's about _power_. And power, dear boy, is something you have to _take_.

**SONNY:**  
I've been taking all night.

**KEWPIE:**  
You've been _destroying_. There's a difference. And I wonder... does Lucy know? Does she know what you've become in her name?

_A beat. SONNY's resolve flickers._

**KEWPIE:**  
Every broken body. Every brutal finish. Was that love, Sonny? Or was that ego?

**SONNY:**  
_(quiet, then steeling)_  
You want to talk? Or you want to bowl?

**KEWPIE:**  
_(grinning)_  
Let's roll.

_Title card: "KINGPIN VS. KING."_

_The fight is different here — more formal, more choreographed, like a boxing match mixed with a dance. They are equals. They are enemies. They are the same._

_It goes back and forth. KEWPIE is strong. SONNY is desperate._

_SONNY sees it: his ball launcher, modified into an over-the-shoulder rocket launcher. He'd dropped it earlier. Forgotten._

_He dives for it. KEWPIE laughs._

**KEWPIE:**  
What are you gonna do, Sonny? Bowl me to death?

_SONNY rises. Red-eyed. Something broken inside him. Or maybe something finally set free._

_The violence that follows is... difficult._

_Shadow puppetry: The launcher. The insertion. The trigger. Balls filling the body. The inflation. The explosion._

_Sound: Too much sound. Then silence._

_Lights up._

_SONNY stands alone. Covered in... everything._

_Above, on a balcony: LUCY. Watching. Her face a mask of horror._

**LUCY:**  
_(whispered)_  
Sonny... what have you done?

_She runs._

_SONNY runs after her._

---

## Scene 11: The Labyrinth

_Room after room. Each one stranger than the last. Sadistic toys. Masochistic furnishings. Kewpie's private collection of excess._

_SONNY moves through them. Slower. Slower._

_He's tired. So tired._

_Each room is a reminder: This is what he was fighting for. This is what she chose._

_Title card: "THE PURSUIT SLOWS."_

_He passes through the labyrinth like a man walking through a dream. Or a nightmare._

_Finally: a door. "EXIT."_

_He opens it._

---

## Scene 12: The End

_The back of the ship. Dawn approaching. The sea stretching forever._

_LUCY stands at the railing. A small single-engine fishing boat is tied alongside._

_SONNY stops. They look at each other._

**SONNY:**  
Lucy.

**LUCY:**  
_(not turning fully)_  
I watched you kill him.

**SONNY:**  
I did it for you.

**LUCY:**  
No. You did it for _you_. Because you couldn't stand losing. Because your ego couldn't take a strike.

**SONNY:**  
That's not—

**LUCY:**  
_(turning, tears streaming)_  
I never asked you to save me, Sonny. I never needed saving. I was making a _choice_. Maybe it was the wrong one, maybe I would have come back, maybe I was just... testing, seeing if you were what I wanted, but now—

_She gestures at him. The blood. The violence written on his body._

**LUCY:**  
Now I know exactly what you are.

**SONNY:**  
I'm the man who loves you.

**LUCY:**  
_(shaking her head)_  
You're the man who destroyed a city for his pride. That's not love. That's possession.

_She climbs over the railing. Into the fishing boat._

**SONNY:**  
Lucy. Wait. LUCY!

_She starts the engine. Casts off._

**LUCY:**  
_(fading into the distance)_  
Goodbye, Sonny. I hope the trophy was worth it.

_The boat pulls away. Her sobs fade into the wind._

_SONNY stands at the railing. Alone. The sun rises. It should be beautiful. It isn't._

_Title card descends, final: "AND THE LANES STAY EMPTY."_

_SONNY sinks to his knees. The ball-shining towel slips from his fingers. Falls into the sea._

_A single spotlight. Just him. The weight of everything he's done._

**SONNY:**  
_(to no one)_  
I just wanted to be the one she chose.

_Silence._

_Then: The sound of pins falling. Distant. A perfect strike._

_But no one's cheering._

_Slow fade to black._

# THE END

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Violence & Shadow Puppetry

All boss "fatalities" should be performed via shadow puppetry upstage, with Foley artists visible stage left creating the sounds. This creates distance from the violence while heightening its impact — we see the shape of horror, hear the sounds, but never the explicit act. This is crucial for maintaining the production's dark comedy tone while allowing the audience to process the escalating brutality.

### Silent Film Aesthetic

Title cards should descend from the flies on physical boards, piano accompaniment should underscore all action sequences, and actors (especially in crowd scenes) should employ exaggerated silent-film gesture and expression. Consider intermittent moments of actors moving in "undercranked" style (unnaturally fast) to evoke early cinema.

### The Crab

Charlie the Crab is the production's most technically demanding costume. Consider a two-person operation: one actor in the crab body, one puppeteer operating the mouse from within. The comedy of this character should be pushed — the mouse is clearly in control while the crab is lovably stupid.

### Lucy's Journey

Though Lucy appears briefly in most scenes, her arc is crucial. Each appearance should show subtle shifts: from willing escape, to genuine uncertainty, to growing horror, to final moral clarity. In the end, she is the only character who has a real awakening — Sonny's "victory" is actually his complete moral defeat, and Lucy is the one who sees it.

### The Ending

The final image should linger. No bows music. Just silence and light fading. Give the audience time to sit with what they've witnessed: a love story that was never actually about love.
